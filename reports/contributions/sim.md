## 3. Open limitations
Full text in `sim/README.md` → LIMITATIONS (mirrored in the T3 row):
1. RM3100 values are datasheet typicals, not per-unit; T1 FACTSHEET still DRAFT; non-table cycle counts `[DERIVED-APPROX]`/PROVISIONAL; board circuit may differ from PNI test conditions.
2. PNI documents disagree on range (±800 vs ±1100 µT); sim uses ±800 µT with hard clipping (real sensor goes non-monotonic).
3. White Gaussian noise only: no 1/f, temperature drift, hysteresis, linearity or cross-axis error (misalignment and Gauss-Markov bias exist but are off by default).
4. Earth field uniform and static; no WMM/IGRF, no secular/diurnal variation; defaults are not our location.
5. IMU noise/bias are generic MEMS guesses (ASSUMED); no vibration, linear/centripetal acceleration, temperature or scale errors.
6. Interference idealised: constant hard iron, fixed soft-iron matrix on Earth field only, motor field ∝ one scalar current; coefficients ASSUMED.
7. Ideal timing: no bus latency, jitter, drops or mag/IMU skew; no transport effects.
8. `fc_attitude_deg` is a crude stand-in, not a heading reference.
9. GPS and true-north fusion out of scope for T3.
10. Accel convention fixed (specific force, level z = −1 g); Firmware Specialist still to confirm the Matek FC's native sign.
11. `calibration_dance` needs ≥ 30 s for good coverage; the 10 s example is a format sample only.
12. Delivered modes come from source code, not hardware: ideal 80/25 Hz grids, AP_Periph calibration/rotation assumed identity, FC-side processing not modelled, int16 overflow clipped and flagged.
13. Spec check: 36 PASS, 0 MISMATCH; 44 keys N/A; spec temperature terms and random-walk bias not modelled. (TC19 resolved by `64579d9`.)
14. DroneCAN mode models the **deprecated** `uavcan.equipment.ahrs.MagneticFieldStrength` (1001) because AP_Periph sends it; `MagneticFieldStrength2` and HiRes not modelled.
15. MSP ≤ 25 Hz is **inferred** from AP_Periph code, not verified.
16. Module firmware on our CAN-L4-3100 is an owner-only unknown, tracked by the PM as blocker **B2** (`BLOCKERS.md`); it decides whether MSP exists and whether the 25 Hz cap applies.
